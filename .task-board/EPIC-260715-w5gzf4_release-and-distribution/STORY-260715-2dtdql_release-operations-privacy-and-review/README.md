# Release operations, privacy and legal compliance, and App Review submission

## Description
Create the operational control plane and evidence package that promotes verified relay, macOS, and iOS candidates, publishes truthful privacy and legal material, supports review with reproducible self-hosted SSH setup, submits the iOS app, and permits safe rollback or response.

## Scope
In scope: release version manifest and go or no-go control, role separation and approvals, cross-platform release and rollback runbooks, current Apple guidance revalidation, public privacy policy and in-app parity, App Privacy labels, export-compliance and regional VPN licensing or storefront decisions, support and retention statements, App Review data-flow and entitlement-purpose evidence, degraded-mode and system-exclusion disclosure, user-owned SSH exit explanation, non-production review host or reproducible setup, review account or profile handling, metadata and screenshot evidence, release-candidate matrix, behavior-to-claim traceability, submission, response handling, incident and credential revocation, and durable audit evidence. Out of scope: inventing legal advice, changing product behavior to match unsupported claims, sharing production credentials, launching unsupported regions, implementing tunnel features, or bypassing negative Gate A0, P0, signing, TestFlight, privacy, or regression evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A versioned release manifest and runbook identify candidate commits and artifacts, owners, approvals, required CI gates, promotion order, communication, retention, abort criteria, rollback, revocation, and post-release checks for relay, macOS, and iOS. 2. A currently dated primary-source review plus accountable product, privacy, legal, security, and regional approvals produce public policy, App Privacy labels, export-compliance answers, retention or deletion commitments, and storefront availability that match actual behavior. 3. The App Review package accurately explains the VPN data path, Network Extension purpose, user-owned SSH exit, observer boundaries, system exclusions, full and degraded modes, diagnostics, support data, and regional limits without claiming anonymity or a Relux-operated exit service. 4. Reviewers receive a reproducible test profile or isolated SSH host and instructions that exercise the submitted binary without exposing private production credentials; access is scoped, monitored for abuse, rotated, and removed after review. 5. The exact signed candidates pass the supported simulator, physical iPhone, Apple-silicon Mac, accessibility, privacy, leak, lifecycle, entitlement, notarization, TestFlight, artifact, migration, install, upgrade, and rollback matrix, with failures blocking promotion. 6. Submission metadata, screenshots, privacy and support URLs, binary and build identifiers, reviewer notes, communications, rejections, corrections, and resubmissions are preserved and traceable to the reviewed behavior and evidence revision.
