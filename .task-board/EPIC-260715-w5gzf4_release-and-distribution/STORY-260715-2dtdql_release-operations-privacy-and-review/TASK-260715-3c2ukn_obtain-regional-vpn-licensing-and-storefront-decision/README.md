# Obtain the regional VPN licensing and storefront decision

## Description
Gather jurisdiction-specific product and legal input and record an accountable launch decision for every intended App Store storefront, including licensing, disclosure, support, data, and operational obligations or explicit exclusion.

## Scope
In scope: intended launch countries or regions, VPN-specific licensing or registration questions, organization eligibility, sanctions and export constraints as directed by counsel, local privacy and retention obligations, consumer and support terms, language or contact requirements, App Store storefront selection, evidence owner, review date, change trigger, exclusion rationale, and implementation handoff. Out of scope: legal advice by engineering, silent worldwide availability, coding geofences without a decision, evading regional law, and storing confidential legal communications beyond approved redacted evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A product owner supplies the intended storefront list and accountable qualified legal or compliance owners record approve, exclude, or needs further action for every entry. 2. Each approved region lists required license, registration, disclosure, privacy, retention, support, export, and operational conditions with evidence and renewal dates. 3. Excluded or unresolved regions map to App Store Connect availability settings and user or support messaging without suggesting the product is available there. 4. The decision is versioned, dated, reviewed before submission, and has change triggers for law, product data flow, telemetry, relay operation, or ownership changes. 5. The task cannot be accepted on an engineering assumption; missing legal input or an unresolved required license remains an explicit blocker to affected storefronts and submission.
