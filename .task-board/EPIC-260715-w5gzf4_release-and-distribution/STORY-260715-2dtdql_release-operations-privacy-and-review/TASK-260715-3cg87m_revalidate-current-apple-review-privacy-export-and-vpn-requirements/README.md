# Revalidate current Apple review, privacy, export, and VPN requirements

## Description
Perform a dated primary-source review immediately before release planning is executed, covering Network Extension distribution, VPN App Review Guideline 5.4, privacy manifests and App Privacy, required-reason APIs, encryption and export compliance, review metadata, and current submission mechanics.

## Scope
In scope: official Apple Developer and App Store Connect sources, access dates and revisions, organization and VPN requirements, entitlement purpose, data-use and retention statements, privacy manifest and label rules, required-reason APIs, encryption questions, regional storefront controls, age rating and content rights, review credentials and notes, screenshots, support and privacy URLs, submission states, and revalidation triggers. Out of scope: relying on blogs as authority, legal advice, contacting Apple under Gate A0 unless required by its owner, product implementation, and treating point-in-time findings as permanent.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A TASK-ID-scoped dossier links direct official sources, access dates, relevant requirement paraphrases, affected task IDs, and facts versus interpretations. 2. A traceability table covers Network Extension purpose, organization status, VPN data use, user-owned SSH exit, system exclusions, degraded mode, diagnostics, support export, privacy labels and manifests, required-reason APIs, encryption, regional availability, metadata, reviewer access, and submission. 3. Changes from the supplied 2026-07-15 evidence are highlighted with impact, owner, deadline, and whether they block archive, TestFlight, or submission. 4. Ambiguous or conflicting official guidance is escalated through an accountable legal, App Review, or Apple channel rather than resolved by unsupported assumption. 5. Another researcher can reproduce the review from the recorded sources and a release check enforces the dossier freshness window.
