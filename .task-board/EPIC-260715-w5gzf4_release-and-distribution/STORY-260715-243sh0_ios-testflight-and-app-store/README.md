# iOS archive, TestFlight, and App Store binary delivery

## Description
Deliver a protected iOS distribution path for ReluxProxyIOS and its packet-tunnel extension, from release signing and archive inspection through TestFlight upload, named-device lifecycle validation, App Store validation, provenance, and build withdrawal or supersession.

## Scope
In scope: Apple Distribution and App Store Connect credential ownership, distribution profiles, host and extension identifiers and entitlements, archive and export configuration, relay and compliance-resource embedding, semantic and build version propagation, embedded-profile and entitlement inspection, TestFlight upload and processing, internal tester rollout, physical iPhone installation and VPN lifecycle checks, privacy manifest and export-compliance validation, symbols, App Store binary preflight, source-to-archive provenance, signed-build reproducibility boundaries, and rollback or supersession runbooks. Out of scope: Developer ID and notarization, public App Store marketing or legal copy owned by the review story, product feature implementation, simulator-only acceptance, live production credentials in tests, and claiming byte-identical signed IPAs when Apple signing introduces nondeterminism.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A clean release archive embeds exactly the approved iOS tunnel extension and required relay or compliance resources, uses matching distribution profiles, and passes automated bundle ID, team, App Group, Keychain, packet-tunnel entitlement, version, and embedded-profile inspection. 2. Protected CI allocates a monotonic build number, exports the declared App Store method, uploads to App Store Connect, waits for processing, records identifiers, and distributes the same candidate to the intended TestFlight group without exposing credentials. 3. A named physical iPhone installs the TestFlight build and passes first launch, privacy and permission gates, extension start and stop, full or degraded capability, app termination, sleep or wake, network change, diagnostics, and uninstall acceptance. 4. The candidate passes App Store validation for architecture, symbols, privacy manifests, required-reason APIs where applicable, export compliance, bundle metadata, and prohibited private APIs. 5. Evidence binds source commit, dependency locks, generator and Xcode versions, relay manifest, protocol version, marketing and build versions, archive and export checksums, profiles, signing identities, and App Store Connect build ID while distinguishing reproducible unsigned inputs from signed outputs. 6. Expiring, withdrawing, superseding, or rejecting a TestFlight build and rotating or revoking credentials follows a tested runbook without corrupting version history or promoting an unverified binary.
