## 2026-07-15 — Reproducibility boundary differs by artifact

Apple-signed archives, profiles, and timestamps introduce expected signed-output variation, so macOS and iOS tasks preserve reproducible source, locks, generated project, relay bytes, unsigned inputs, and exact signed-output provenance rather than making an invalid byte-identical IPA claim.

This boundary avoids circular release dependencies.
